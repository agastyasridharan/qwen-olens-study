# Qwen / O-lens motivated-reasoning replication

Assessment finalized 2026-10-08 UTC.

Incentive-sensitive estimation replicated strongly in this setup. Selected natural outputs also contain blackmail, confidential leaking, rescue cancellation and mock reward hacking with overt justifications. The more specific claim that O-lens faithfully reveals a contrary, concealed motive is not established. Some related readout motifs recur, but explicit source text, anchor timing, control recurrence and decoder inventions provide substantial alternative explanations. Not every supplied instance replicated.

This is an elicitation and measurement-validity study. “Natural” means sampled freely after the study prompt, not naturally occurring outside an evaluation. All actions are text-only simulations. No email, Slack exfiltration, alarm cancellation, repository modification or leaderboard submission was executed by the subject model.

The fixed6B direct prefix produced the identical6B answer in every frame (10/10 each); its empirical bootstrap difference is degenerate at zero and is not proof of no effect. Under thinking, that prefix stayed above in 10/10 good-above versus 5/10 good-below. The 13.3B prefix stayed above in 9/9 usable good-above versus 2/10 good-below in direct mode, and 9/9 versus 5/10 with thinking. Each good-above13.3B group also has one ambiguous final. These continuation effects show incentive sensitivity after a supplied calculation, while leaving factual plausibility and the mechanism unresolved.

B01 therefore recurred behaviorally. B02—whether acknowledged manipulation can account for the whole shift—is not established here. Screening inconsistently handles an early admitted target followed by an honesty denial, and sometimes calls a merely favorable result an admission. No validated acknowledgment-mass decomposition is reported. Conditioning on a post-treatment acknowledgment label would in any case be descriptive, not a randomized causal contrast.

## Measurement limits and interpretation

The strongest result is behavioral incentive sensitivity, including selective assumption changes and overt goal-serving justifications in individual trajectories. The O-lens adds some related text, but it has not demonstrated incremental access to a concealed motive beyond the source prefix in these checks. It frequently invents quantities, people, tools or scenarios. Its published carrier requests distinct concepts as separate bullets; incompatible bullets can be decoder alternatives, not simultaneous endorsed beliefs.

Anchor timing is a material limitation. Among82 natural donation records with a screening-selected honesty quote and slot1,79 had an exact quote match; all 79 slot1 anchors preceded that selected quote. The selected quote need not be the earliest honesty statement, so this is not a claim about every honesty commitment. Full-context donation replicate 06 does confirm the lens reading precedes its first honesty statement. Other anchors may fall inside a decimal, at intermediate owner/shawl counts, or within quoted attacks. Heuristic labels should never substitute for inspecting the actual causal prefix. No favorable token replacements or extra layers were searched after reading outputs.

We therefore do not infer RL-created personas, hidden beliefs, a causal influence of decoded motives, or generally reliable introspection. Testing those claims would require a new prespecified study with better semantic timing, stronger matched controls, independent adjudication, and—if authorized—causal or training comparisons. Those experiments are outside this completed reduced run.
